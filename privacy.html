Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026 (writing on set work added)

Ghelli Study Hub (ghelli-study-hub.pages.dev) is run by Abdullah Ghelli, who also runs Ghelli tutoring, an online tutoring service based in Karachi, Pakistan. This page explains what the site records about you, why, and what you can do about it. If anything here is unclear, message us on WhatsApp (+92 332 3332003).

Using the site without signing in

Most of the site needs no account. If you don't sign in, we don't know who you are.

  • No adverts, no analytics and no tracking cookies.
  • Things you choose on the site (your specs, light or dark theme, how you marked questions, saved worksheets, bookmarks) are kept in your own browser's storage. They are never sent to us, and clearing your browser's site data removes them.
  • The site's fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees your device's IP address when the page loads. The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which handles every request to it and keeps short-lived security logs.

Signing in

Signing in is for students and teachers of Ghelli tutoring. Teachers use it to set work, and students use it to see what's due and record how they got on. You sign in with your Google account.

What we get from Google: your name, your email address and confirmation that Google has verified the email. We don't get your password, and we can't see your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account.

What we store when you're signed in:

  • Your name and email address, and which teacher you're a student of.
  • The work your teacher sets you, and the dates it's due.
  • How you mark each question in that work (Got it, Partly, Missed it or Skipped), any scores you enter for past papers, and when you started and finished.
  • Anything you write on the questions of your set work (on the question and in the writing space below it), so your teacher can see your answers. Writing anywhere else, including notes on papers in the paper viewer, stays in your browser and isn't sent to us.
  • When you last used the site while signed in.
  • A sign-in cookie (called gsh_s) that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It's strictly necessary for signing in and isn't used for anything else. We store only a scrambled (hashed) copy of it, so even we can't use it to sign in as you.

Students can sign in only after a teacher adds their email address. A teacher adding you is how your name and email first get onto the site.

Why we use it, and who sees it

  • We use your information only so your teacher can set you work and see how you're getting on, and to keep you signed in.
  • You can see your own work and marks. Your teacher can see your name, email, the work they've set you, your marks, what you wrote on set work and when you were last active. Abdullah Ghelli, as the site's owner, can see everything stored on the site.
  • We never sell your information, never use it for advertising and never share it with anyone else, except the services that run the site (below) or if the law requires it.

Services that help run the site

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the database where sign-in information is stored. Cloudflare may process data in data centres outside your country.
  • Google handles signing in. When you sign in, Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account.

How long we keep it

  • We keep your account while you're a student of ours.
  • When a teacher removes you, your account and everything recorded for it are deleted straight away.
  • You can delete your own account at any time from My work (choose Delete my account). This also deletes your work, marks and writing.
  • Sign-in sessions end when you sign out or after 30 days.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply within 30 days. Wherever you live, we'll handle these requests the same way.

Children

Many of our students are under 18. A student under 13, or under the age of digital consent where they live, should sign in only with a parent's or guardian's permission. Google requires children under 13 to use an account supervised by a parent (Family Link). A parent or guardian can ask us at any time to see or delete their child's information.

Keeping it safe

The whole site uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The sign-in cookie can't be read by scripts on the page, and requests that change anything are accepted only from the site itself. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we store to the minimum needed.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects what we do with your information, we'll tell students and teachers before it takes effect.
